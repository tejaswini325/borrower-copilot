# Three run-throughs

The app contains one-click demo buttons for all three challenge borrowers. The outputs below are generated by the same rules in `app.js`. Amounts are indicative, not lender promises.

## Priya — 29, salaried MNC engineer

**Questions/path:** purpose → personal loan → ₹8L → ₹1.10L net income → salaried → ₹14k existing EMI → ₹45k household expenses → age 29 → score 780 → stability high → savings 3–5 months → no bounce → fee 2% → quote 12%.

**Reasoning:** strong score and stable salary improve the lender-like boundary and rate; the existing ₹14k EMI reduces new EMI headroom. Household expenses also constrain the safe ceiling.

**Outputs:** Borrow if the ₹8L request remains within the safe boundary; fair rate is toward the lower end of the salaried personal band; compare the 12% quote against the displayed fair band and all-in APR; EMI ceiling is the safe EMI, not the lender maximum.

**Negotiation card:** target the displayed fair-rate band, ask for KFS/APR and fees, and refuse to let a larger sanction dictate the EMI.

## Ravi — 42, kirana owner

**Questions/path:** business purpose → LAP route → ₹15L → conservative ₹35k monthly income → self-employed → ₹0 existing EMI → ₹28k expenses → age 42 → score unknown → medium stability → savings 3–5 months → no bounce → ₹45L collateral → variable income 21–50% → fee 1.5% → quote 13%.

**Reasoning:** the property makes a secured/business route worth exploring, but cash-flow affordability still drives the safe amount. Unknown score widens pricing uncertainty.

**Outputs:** route toward secured/business credit rather than generic unsecured personal credit; use the safe amount/EMI boundary; compare the 13% offer with the fair band and ask the lender to show valuation/LTV assumptions plus APR.

**Negotiation card:** “Please price this as a secured/business loan and show me the KFS, APR, fees, total repayment and collateral/LTV assumptions.”

## Anita — 35, gig + tailoring

**Questions/path:** vehicle purpose → two-wheeler → ₹1.5L → ₹28k typical income → informal/gig → ₹9k existing EMI → ₹18k expenses → age 35 → score unknown → medium stability → no savings → recent bounce → variable income >50% → fee 2.5% → quote 30%.

**Reasoning:** existing debt, a recent bounce, high variable income and no emergency buffer sharply reduce safe headroom and widen price uncertainty.

**Outputs:** Borrow less or Don’t borrow depending on the calculated safe headroom; the 30% quote is clearly a warning versus the prototype fair band; safe EMI is intentionally low; stress shows further deterioration.

**Negotiation card:** do not negotiate only on the amount. Ask for total repayment/APR, consider a smaller scooter loan and address high-cost existing app debt first.
